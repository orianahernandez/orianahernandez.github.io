# Purpose and Scope 

## Purpose 

GradPath is an academic planning tool/ UC scheduler that helps University of Cincinnati students generate personalized graduation plans using degree audit information and course catalog data. The system assists students in understanding their remaining degree requirements and developing a path toward graduation while considering prerequisites, course availability, and individual academic preferences. 

## Scope 

The scope of GradPath includes degree audit upload and analysis, graduation plan generation, prerequisite validation, course scheduling recommendations, transfer credit consideration, and support for exploring additional majors or minors. Students may review and modify generated plans to reflect personal preferences and academic goals. 

The scope of this project does not include course registration, modification of UC's university records or replacement of academic advising services. Generated plans serve as planning recommendations. Must be reviewed by an academic advisor to do final decisions.

# MoSCoW Summary 

## Must 

**REQ-01:** The system shall allow a student to load a degree audit file (Supported PDF format) 

**REQ-02:** The system shall extract completed, in-progress, and remaining courses from a valid degree audit. 

**REQ-03:** The system shall generate and display a semester-by-semester graduation plan using degree requirements, prerequisite information, and course availability data that can be reviewed by the student and shared with the academic advisor. 

**REQ-04:** The system shall allow students to modify generated plans by adding, removing, or moving courses. 

**REQ-08:** The system shall retrieve and maintain current course catalog information, including course descriptions, pre-requisites, semester availability from publicly available University of Cincinnati course catalog sources. 

**NFR-01** : The system shall generate a graduation plan within 30 seconds for 95% of valid degree audit submissions. 

**NFR-02:** The system shall process degree audit files locally in the browser and shall not permanently store degree audit information on the server. 

**NFR-04** : The system shall successfully process at least 85% of valid degree audit uploads without failure. 

## Should 

**REQ-05:** The system shall evaluate the effect of major or minor changes on a student's graduation plan. 

**REQ-06:** The system shall evaluate the effect of transfer credit incorporation on a student's graduation plan. 

**REQ-07:** The system shall generate a shareable link that allows an academic advisor to review a student's graduation plan. 

**NFR-03:** A first-time user shall be able to generate a graduation plan in under 10 minutes without assistance. 

**NFR-5 :** The system shall provide accessible user interactions and support students with accessibility needs, including keyboard-accessible schedule editing functionality 

## Could 

None Identified for this release for now. 

## Won't 

Direct integration with catalyst 

Automatic course registration 

Student Accounts with login 

# Inherited Constraints 

CON-01- Economic 

GradPath must be developed and deployed using free or open-source technologies and free-tier cloud resources, resulting in limitations on storage, processing capacity, and scalability. 

## CON-02- Professional 

GradPath may only use publicly available academic information and student-provided degree audits unless additional access is approved by university personnel. 

## CON-03- Security 

GradPath shall not permanently store loaded degree audits and shall only retain uploaded files for the duration required to generate a plan. No accounts will be required to store information. 

## CON-04- Ethical 

GradPath shall be presented as an advising aid and not as a replacement for academic advising. Students must be encouraged to verify plans with an academic advisor. 

# Assumptions and Open Questions 

**Assumption 1:** Course data is publicly available and accurate in the University of Cincinnati's Course catalog for the GradPath team to verify course pre-requisite, semester availability, and general course information. 

**Assumption 2** : Students will review generated plans before making academic decisions, and Academic advisors remain responsible for final approval of academic plans. 

**Assumption 3** : Degree Audit files contain sufficient information to determine completed, in-progress, and remaining requirements. 

**Assumption 4:** What-if audits follow a format that can be processed to normal audits 

**Open Question 1** : Are the current co-op scheduling rules accurate for all supported degree programs? Answer provided by advisor EET single rotation, CS double rotation, Aerospace-> will check 

**Open Question 2:** Does the UC what-if degree audit support minor planning requirements for all supported programs? 

**Open Question 3** : Which University of Cincinnati colleges and degree programs are included in the scope of the first release?
